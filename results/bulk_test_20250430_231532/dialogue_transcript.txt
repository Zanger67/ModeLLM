- By incorporating flexibility for developing countries and emphasizing climate finance, the proposal seeks to garner broader support and promote cooperation among nations with differing economic and developmental realities.
- The focus on market-based solutions and private-sector engagement fosters political viability by appealing to the interests of diverse stakeholders and promoting innovative approaches to addressing climate change.
