Title: Title: Global Climate Action Proposal: Implementing a Sustainable Global Carbon Tax
Action Items:

1. Establish a Global Carbon Tax Framework:
- Create a global carbon tax framework that sets a uniform price on carbon emissions, with differentiated rates based on countries' economic capabilities and historical emissions.
- Design the framework to be flexible, allowing countries to choose between direct taxation or a cap-and-trade system based on their specific circumstances and preferences.

2. Allocate Climate Finance to Developing Countries:
- Allocate a portion of the revenue generated from the global carbon tax to support developing countries in their transition to clean energy sources and adaptation efforts.
- Provide financial assistance, technology transfer, and capacity building programs to enhance the resilience of developing nations to climate impacts.

Considering Different National Perspectives:

- Acknowledge the principle of "common but differentiated responsibilities," recognizing historical emissions and economic capabilities when determining each country's contribution to the global carbon tax.
- Provide flexibility in implementation to address concerns of developing nations like India, ensuring that the tax does not disproportionately burden their economies while promoting sustainable development.

This proposal aims to strike a balance between environmental protection and economic growth by implementing a global carbon tax that incentivizes emissions reductions, supports clean energy innovation, and fosters international cooperation in combating climate change. It respects the perspectives of various nations while providing a practical and politically viable pathway towards achieving our shared climate goals.
