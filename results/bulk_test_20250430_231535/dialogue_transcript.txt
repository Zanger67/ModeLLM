Title: Title: Global Carbon Tax Initiative: Promoting Equitable Climate Action
Action Items:
1. Implement a progressive global carbon tax that differentiates between developed and developing nations based on historical emissions and current economic capacities. Developed nations will bear a higher tax burden to reflect their historical contributions to carbon emissions, while also providing financial support to assist developing nations in their transition to cleaner energy sources.
2. Establish a transparent framework for carbon pricing, ensuring revenues collected from the tax are directed towards funding renewable energy projects, climate adaptation measures, and technology transfer initiatives in developing countries. Encourage innovation and investment in clean technologies by creating incentives for businesses to reduce emissions and transition towards sustainable practices.
3. Foster international cooperation and mutual accountability through regular reporting and review mechanisms to assess progress towards emission reduction targets. Encourage dialogue among nations to exchange best practices, promote knowledge sharing, and address any challenges that may arise in the implementation of the global carbon tax.

Considering National Perspectives:
- Developed Nations: Acknowledge historical responsibilities and commit to bearing a greater share of the carbon tax burden to support global climate goals.
- Developing Nations: Receive financial assistance and technology transfer support to facilitate a just transition towards low-carbon development while safeguarding economic growth.
- Economically Diverse Nations: Ensure that the global carbon tax is implemented in a flexible and equitable manner that considers the diverse economic capacities and developmental needs of countries worldwide.

Political Viability:
This proposal strikes a balance between environmental urgency and economic realities, promoting a collaborative approach that respects national sovereignty while advancing collective climate action. By addressing historical inequities, fostering innovation, and promoting cooperation, this initiative aims to garner broad support and drive meaningful progress in combating climate change on a global scale.

Title: Title: Proposal for Equitable Implementation of a Global Carbon Tax
Action Items:
1. Differentiated Tax Rates: Implement a global carbon tax with differentiated tax rates based on countries' historical emissions, economic development levels, and capacity to mitigate carbon emissions effectively. Developed nations with higher historical emissions and advanced economic capacities would face higher tax rates, while developing nations with lower emissions and limited resources would be subject to more gradual increases.

2. Climate Finance Mechanism: Establish a dedicated climate finance mechanism to support developing nations in their transition to cleaner technologies and sustainable practices. This mechanism would provide financial assistance, technology transfer, and capacity-building initiatives to ensure that all countries can meet their emission reduction targets while maintaining economic growth.

3. Review and Adjustment Mechanism: Create a periodic review and adjustment mechanism to assess the effectiveness and fairness of the global carbon tax system. This mechanism would allow for regular evaluations of countries' emission reduction efforts, economic progress, and capacity-building measures, enabling adjustments to be made to ensure the equitable distribution of responsibilities and benefits.

Considering Different Perspectives:
- Developed Nations: Address concerns about economic competitiveness and ensure that tax rates are proportionate to historical emissions to promote fairness and accountability.
- Developing Nations: Provide support through the climate finance mechanism to facilitate a just transition to a low-carbon economy without hindering economic growth.
- Diplomatic Committee: Facilitate dialogue and cooperation among nations to build consensus on the implementation of the global carbon tax, emphasizing the shared responsibility of all countries in addressing climate change.

Political Viability:
This proposal strikes a balance between the diverse perspectives of developed and developing nations, offering a pragmatic and equitable approach to global climate action. By incorporating differentiated tax rates, a climate finance mechanism, and a review system, it ensures that all countries can participate effectively in the transition to a sustainable future while respecting their unique circumstances.
