- All parties will collaborate to strike a balance between environmental objectives and economic considerations, fostering a pragmatic and effective approach to addressing climate change.

Political Viability:

This proposal aims to strike a balance between the interests of developed and developing nations, offering a pragmatic and equitable framework for global cooperation on climate action. By promoting fairness, flexibility, and innovation, it seeks to garner broad support and pave the way for meaningful progress in combating climate change.
