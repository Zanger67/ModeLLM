1. Establish a Global Carbon Tax framework that includes differentiated responsibilities based on national emissions profiles and development stages. 3. Form a Climate Solidarity Alliance to facilitate collaboration among nations, ensuring transparency, accountability, and mutual support in meeting climate targets. The Alliance will provide a platform for knowledge sharing, capacity building, and collective action to address the climate crisis effectively.

Considering Different National Perspectives:
- Developed countries will lead in contributing to the Global Carbon Tax fund and providing financial and technological assistance to developing nations.
- Developing countries will receive support to implement climate actions while gradually increasing their contributions as they progress in their development journey.
- The proposal acknowledges historical responsibilities, different capacities, and the need for a just transition towards a sustainable future for all nations.

Political Viability:
The proposal seeks to strike a balance between ambitious climate action and economic considerations by integrating market-based solutions with equitable frameworks. It respects the principle of common but differentiated responsibilities and offers concrete mechanisms to address climate change collaboratively. The proposal encourages cooperation and solidarity among nations, promoting a shared vision of a sustainable and resilient future.
