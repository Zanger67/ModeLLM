Action Item 3:

This collaborative effort will prioritize research and development in clean energy technologies to accelerate the global transition to a greener economy.

- The establishment of the Climate Finance Fund addresses the needs of developing countries, ensuring they receive support in their efforts to combat climate change while promoting a sense of global solidarity.
- Emphasizing technology transfer and innovation appeals to nations valuing technological advancement, aligning with the interests of countries focused on economic growth and sustainability.

Political Viability:
This proposal strikes a balance between the interests of developed and developing nations, offering a pragmatic and inclusive approach to climate action. By addressing concerns about fairness, providing financial support, and promoting innovation, this framework is politically viable and sets a path towards global cooperation in combating climate change.
