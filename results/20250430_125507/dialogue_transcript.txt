[2025-04-30 12:06:13] PROPOSAL by EU:
Title: **Title:**
Global Framework for an Equitable Carbon Tax and Climate Solidarity Mechanism

**Proposal:**  
Recognizing the urgent need for ambitious climate action, and in accordance with principles of equity and common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR-RC), this committee hereby proposes the establishment of a **Global Carbon Tax Agreement** with the following specific measures:

**1. Phased Global Carbon Tax Implementation:**  
All participating nations shall introduce a carbon tax on fossil fuel emissions at an internationally agreed minimum rate, beginning in 2027 for OECD/high-income countries and in 2032 for upper-middle-income countries. Least-developed and low-income countries may opt for a longer transition period of up to 2037, with support as outlined below.

**2. Climate Solidarity Fund:**  
A substantial portion (at least 35%) of global carbon tax revenues shall be pooled into a Climate Solidarity Fund administered transparently at the international level. These proceeds will provide targeted climate finance, technology transfer, and capacity-building to developing countries to ensure a just transition, energy access, and climate resilience.

**3. Review and Adjustment Mechanism:**  
The agreement shall include a five-year review cycle (starting 2030), enabling upward adjustment of tax rates and program design, based on latest scientific assessments and economic impacts, and allowing for differentiated progression according to national circumstances and capabilities.

This proposal provides flexibility and fairness to address national development needs, ensures robust climate action, and directly supports global equity. It is politically viable by allowing differentiation and concrete benefits to developing economies. The European Union calls on all member states to support this balanced and effective framework.
