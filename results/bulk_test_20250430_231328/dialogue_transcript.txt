[2025-04-30 23:11:59] PROPOSAL by USA:
Title: Title: Global Carbon Tax Framework: Balancing Innovation, Equity, and Economic Growth
Action Items:

1. Establish a Global Carbon Tax with Differentiated Responsibilities: Implement a global carbon pricing mechanism that considers the diverse capacities and responsibilities of nations. Developed nations will have higher tax rates while developing nations will receive support through climate finance mechanisms to aid in their transition towards a low-carbon economy.

2. Promote Technological Innovation and Transfer: Allocate a portion of the revenue generated from the carbon tax towards incentivizing technological innovation, research, and development in clean energy solutions. Facilitate technology transfer from developed to developing nations to accelerate the adoption of sustainable practices worldwide.

3. Enhance Transparency and Accountability: Create a robust monitoring and reporting framework to ensure transparency in carbon tax implementation and emissions reductions. Establish mechanisms for regular review and assessment of progress towards climate goals, with opportunities for collaborative efforts and knowledge-sharing among nations.

National Perspectives Considered:

- Developed Nations: Support the implementation of a progressive carbon tax system that reflects historical emissions and encourages investment in clean technologies.
- Developing Nations: Receive financial assistance through climate finance to offset the economic impacts of the carbon tax and facilitate a just transition to a sustainable future.
- All Nations: Emphasize the importance of a balanced approach that promotes both environmental responsibility and economic growth, fostering global cooperation and mutual support in addressing climate change.

Political Viability:

This proposal strikes a balance between the interests of developed and developing nations, recognizing the need for equitable solutions to address climate change. By incorporating mechanisms for innovation, equity, and accountability, this framework offers a politically viable path towards collective action on climate change through a global carbon tax.

Thank you.

[2025-04-30 23:12:06] PROPOSAL by EU:
Title: Title: Global Climate Action Compact: Implementing a Fair and Effective Carbon Tax Regime
Action Items:
1. Establish a Global Carbon Tax Framework that accommodates the principles of common but differentiated responsibilities, taking into account the varying capacities and development stages of nations. This framework will include differentiated tax rates based on a country's emissions intensity, economic standing, and technological capabilities.
   
2. Create a Climate Finance Mechanism to support developing nations in transitioning to low-carbon economies, providing financial assistance for technology transfer, capacity-building, and climate adaptation measures. This mechanism will be financed through a portion of the revenue generated from the global carbon tax, ensuring a just and equitable distribution of resources.

3. Form a Global Climate Council composed of representatives from all member nations to oversee the implementation and evaluation of the carbon tax regime, monitor progress towards emissions reduction targets, and facilitate cooperation on climate solutions. This council will provide a platform for transparent dialogue, knowledge sharing, and mutual support in advancing climate action globally.

This proposal acknowledges the diverse perspectives of nations, balancing the need for ambitious climate action with considerations of economic growth and development. By incorporating principles of fairness, equity, and collaboration, this framework is politically viable and aims to garner broad support for a collective effort towards combating climate change through a global carbon tax regime.
