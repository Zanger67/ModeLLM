Title: **Title:**
Framework for a Flexible and Fair Global Carbon Pricing Mechanism

**Proposal:**  
Recognizing the urgent need for ambitious yet practical climate action, the undersigned proposes the following approach to a Global Carbon Tax:

**1. Flexible National Carbon Pricing Commitments:**  
All participating nations shall adopt a form of carbon pricing—either a carbon tax or an emissions trading system—to reduce greenhouse gas emissions consistent with their national circumstances. Countries may determine the specific design, implementation timeline, and pricing structure most appropriate for their economic and developmental context, provided that transparency and measurable progress are ensured.

**2. Climate Innovation Fund:**  
A portion of revenues generated by national carbon pricing mechanisms will be pooled into a Climate Innovation Fund, administered by an independent international consortium. This fund will support technology transfer, clean energy infrastructure, and capacity building—prioritizing assistance to developing countries to safeguard sustainable development and economic growth.

**3. Review and Adjustment Mechanism:**  
Every three years, an independent panel will review global progress toward emissions targets and assess the adequacy of national commitments. Recommendations may be issued for adjustment and greater alignment, but national sovereignty in policy implementation will be respected.

**Rationale:**  
This framework accommodates developed economies’ ambition for robust climate action, addresses developing nations’ concerns around equity and growth, and preserves flexibility for national circumstances. It strikes a balance between collective accountability and national sovereignty, fostering genuinely global cooperation while promoting innovation and sustainable development.

**This proposal is specific, measurable, adjustable, and politically viable for a diverse international community.**
