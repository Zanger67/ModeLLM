[2025-04-29 19:52:53] USA:
Distinguished Chair, Honorable Delegates,  

The United States of America recognizes the undeniable reality and urgency of addressing climate change. As one of the largest economies and innovators globally, we are committed to fostering solutions that advance sustainability while ensuring economic growth and shared prosperity.

While market-based mechanisms, like carbon pricing, can be effective tools to reduce emissions, a global one-size-fits-all carbon tax raises concerns about inequitable economic impositions, potential impacts on innovation, and the sovereignty of nations to tailor policies to their unique circumstances.

We are already making significant progress toward decarbonization through the promotion of clean energy technologies, public-private partnerships, and market-driven solutions. We also urge that considerations of fairness be integral to any global framework. While developed nations bear historical responsibility, emerging economies must not disproportionately shoulder burdens that could hinder their development. Instead, we call for collaborative solutions that spur technological advancement, create green jobs, and ensure all parties are equipped to meet ambitious climate goals.  

The United States stands ready to engage constructively to craft pragmatic, effective, and equitable climate policies that secure a sustainable future for all.  

Thank you.

[2025-04-29 19:54:11] PROPOSAL by USA:
Title: **Proposal Title: "Balanced Climate Action Framework: Flexible Global Carbon Pricing Mechanism"**
**Recognizing** the urgency of climate change and the need for immediate action to meet the goals of the Paris Agreement, this proposal seeks to establish a framework for cooperative global climate action through flexible and equitable carbon pricing mechanisms.

**Action Items:**

1. **Adopt a Flexible Carbon Pricing Framework**: Nations will commit to implementing carbon pricing mechanisms (such as a carbon tax or cap-and-trade systems), tailored to their unique economic and developmental circumstances. Each country will design its own system to achieve measurable and verifiable emissions reductions. Developed countries will lead the way with ambitious targets, while accommodating the growth needs of developing nations.

2. **Establish a Global Climate Technology and Financing Partnership**: A multilateral fund will be created, supported by contributions from developed nations, to assist developing countries in implementing low-carbon technologies and advancing their renewable energy sectors. The fund will prioritize capacity building, R&D collaboration, and affordable access to clean technologies.

3. **Set up an Accountability and Collaboration Mechanism**: An independent international body, administered through the UNFCCC, will oversee and report on countries’ progress in a transparent manner. It will ensure that efforts are equitable and aligned with global climate goals while recognizing different levels of responsibility and capability.

**Considerations:**
- This framework respects the principle of “common but differentiated responsibilities” by balancing the historical emissions of developed nations with the developmental needs of emerging economies.
- It recognizes the sovereignty of nations by allowing flexibility in policy design while ensuring accountability and cooperation.
- By aligning carbon pricing with technological support and financing, it encourages global innovation, economic growth, and emissions reductions simultaneously.

This proposal calls on all nations to support this balanced and politically viable path to combat climate change.

**Vote: Yes / No**

India sees strong potential for collaboration in designing robust mechanisms for the disbursement and oversight of climate finance and technology transfer. We propose jointly advocating for a binding annual financial target commensurate with rising ambition and the establishment of a transparent UN-led body to ensure both the fairness and effectiveness of implementation.

We look forward to working with the EU to ensure global climate action advances ambition while safeguarding development and equity for all.
