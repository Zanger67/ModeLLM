[2025-04-30 13:12:49] PROPOSAL by USA:
Title: **Title:**
Balanced Pathways Agreement: A Framework for Global Carbon Pricing and Innovation

**Proposal:**  
Recognizing the urgent need to address climate change while considering the diverse economic circumstances of nations, this committee proposes the following balanced approach:

**1. Gradual, Differentiated Carbon Levy System:**  
Establish a voluntary, tiered global carbon levy, with differentiated rates based on a country's level of development and historical emissions. Developed nations will implement higher base rates, while emerging and developing economies may adopt lower or delayed rates, with support for capacity-building and flexibility to protect economic growth and poverty reduction goals.

**2. International Climate Innovation Fund:**  
Create a pooled fund, financed in part by developed nations’ carbon levy proceeds, to support clean technology research, development, and deployment worldwide. The fund will prioritize technology transfer, capacity-building in least-developed countries, and public-private partnerships, accelerating global access to affordable low-carbon solutions.

**3. Transparent Monitoring and Cooperation Mechanism:**  
Form an international council to oversee transparent reporting of carbon pricing actions, emissions reductions, and fund allocation. All participating nations will submit annual progress reports, with forums for peer review, knowledge-sharing, and technical assistance.

**Rationale:**  
This proposal acknowledges the European Union’s call for robust carbon pricing, China and India’s emphasis on equity, and the United States’ belief in technological innovation and market mechanisms. It provides flexibility for national circumstances while incentivizing global action, innovation, and fairness. 

*We urge delegates to vote in favor of the Balanced Pathways Agreement as a pragmatic, effective climate policy that bridges competing priorities and advances our shared goals.*

[2025-04-30 13:13:03] PROPOSAL by China:
Title: Title: Equitable Global Carbon Tax Framework
Proposal Overview  
This committee proposes a tiered and equitable Carbon Tax Framework that safeguards both our planet and the economic growth of all nations, in particular those at earlier stages of development.

Action Item 1: Tiered Carbon Tax Rates  
• Nations will be classified into tiers based on historic emissions, per capita income, and development status.  
• Developed countries, responsible for the majority of historic emissions, will adopt higher carbon tax rates to reflect their greater capacity and responsibility.  
• Developing nations will adopt more moderate tax rates, preserving room for sustainable industrial growth and poverty reduction.

Action Item 2: Dedicated Climate Finance & Technology Transfer  
• A significant portion of revenues—no less than 30%—collected from the global carbon tax will be allocated to a dedicated Climate Fund.  
• This Fund will support technology transfer, capacity-building, and clean energy infrastructure in developing countries, ensuring that the transition to low-carbon pathways is accessible to all.

Action Item 3: Transparent Governance and Review  
• A global oversight body will be established to monitor tax revenues, ensure transparent disbursement of funds, and regularly review tier classifications.  
• The framework will be reassessed every five years to reflect evolving economic capacities, technological advancements, and emission trends.

Rationale and Expected Outcome  
By aligning the tax with differentiated responsibilities and dedicating resources to technological empowerment, this proposal offers a balanced, politically viable pathway that respects both the global imperative to reduce emissions and the development needs of emerging economies.
