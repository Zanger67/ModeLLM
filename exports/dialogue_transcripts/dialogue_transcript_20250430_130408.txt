[2025-04-30 12:57:56] PROPOSAL by USA:
Title: **Title:**
Framework for Flexible, Market-Based Global Carbon Pricing

**Preamble:**  
Recognizing the urgent need for decisive international action on climate change, and mindful of the diverse economic conditions, development priorities, and historical responsibilities of member states, this committee proposes a flexible framework for Global Carbon Pricing that balances ambition, innovation, and equity.

**Action Items:**  

1. **Nationally Determined Carbon Pricing Mechanism**  
All member countries agree to implement a form of explicit carbon pricing—such as a carbon tax, emissions trading scheme, or equivalent market-based mechanism—by 2028. The structure, rate, and use of revenues will remain the sovereign choice of each nation, reflecting unique economic and developmental contexts.

2. **Technology Innovation and Transfer Fund**  
A joint international fund will be established to accelerate clean technology R&D, and to facilitate affordable technology transfer and capacity-building for developing economies. Contributions will be differentiated: developed countries will provide the majority of funding, with participation from advanced emerging economies encouraged.

3. **Transparency and Technical Cooperation Platform**  
Signatories will participate in an annual reporting and peer review process, coordinated by an independent international body, to share experiences, measure emissions reductions, and enable voluntary cooperation—such as linking carbon markets or exchanging best practices—while respecting national circumstances.

**Conclusion:**  
This framework aims to advance rapid action on climate mitigation through flexible, nationally determined approaches, strong support for technology innovation, and meaningful transparency. It respects the needs and rights of both developed and developing countries, and offers a politically viable pathway to global emissions reductions.  

We urge all delegations to vote in favor.

This approach balances ambitious climate goals with fairness, offering nations the flexibility needed for sustainable development. It recognizes the shared responsibility of combating climate change while addressing disparities in historical emissions and economic capacity.

Title: Title: Equitable Carbon Pricing for Global Climate Stewardship
Proposal:

1. Implement a Global Carbon Tax (GCT): We hereby propose the adoption of a flexible, tiered Global Carbon Tax based on each country's GDP per capita and historical emissions. This ensures that higher-income nations bear a proportionally greater responsibility, acknowledging their significant contribution to greenhouse gas (GHG) concentrations and capacity to invest in low-carbon technologies. The revenue generated from this tax will be allocated primarily towards international climate mitigation efforts. 

2. Establish a Climate Fund: Revenue generated by the GCT will be directed into a dedicated International Climate Fund for vulnerable and developing nations. This fund will be employed for initiatives like climate adaption projects, creation of jobs in green industries, and mobilizing climate-friendly infrastructural developments.

3. Review and Adjust: To ascertain the viability and fairness of the GCT, a comprehensive review shall take place every five years. Adjustments on the carbon pricing will be made on a per country basis based on progress made towards respective NDCs (Nationally Determined Contributions), and the degree to which economies have been impacted by the tax, particularly for developing nations.

In respect to diverse national perspectives and total political viability, this proposal takes into account equitable distribution of responsibilities in accordance with socioeconomic contexts, thus aiming to reduce GHG emissions effectively and fairly. This proposal paves a balanced path, combining aggressive climate action with considerate socioeconomic considerations. 

The EU appeals to fellow nations to endorse this proposal in the spirit of shared responsibility and towards the greater good of planetary health.
