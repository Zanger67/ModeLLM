[2025-04-30 13:39:34] PROPOSAL by USA:
Title: TITLE: Global Carbon Tax for Sustainable and Equitable Growth
PROPOSAL:
1. Phased Implementation with a Flexible Rate System  
   • Establish a globally coordinated carbon tax starting with a modest floor price (e.g., US$25 per metric ton) that increases gradually over five-year intervals.  
   • Incorporate differentiated timelines for developing nations, reflecting the principle of Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities, ensuring they have the flexibility to meet developmental needs while reducing emissions.  

2. Equitable Support & Safeguards  
   • Create a Climate Innovation Fund, financed partly by carbon-tax revenues, to facilitate technology transfer, research grants, and capacity-building in low- and middle-income countries.  
   • Develop transitional allowances or border adjustment measures to protect industries vulnerable to competitiveness risks, preventing economic disruption while still incentivizing emissions reductions.  

3. Transparent Monitoring & Accountability  
   • Establish an independent international body to ensure uniform reporting, verification, and oversight, providing transparency to all stakeholders.  
   • Mandate regular global assessments of the tax’s economic, social, and environmental impacts with opportunities to adjust rate trajectories and support measures, preserving fairness and efficacy.  

RATIONALE:  
This proposal balances the urgency of climate action with economic realities. A gradually increasing tax guides innovation and lowers emissions without undermining growth. Differentiated timelines alongside robust financial and technological support help developing countries transition fairly, while built-in safeguards protect industry competitiveness. Harmonized monitoring ensures accountability, fosters trust, and enables collective adaptation. A global carbon tax operating under these conditions is politically viable, economically sound, and crucial for preserving our planet’s future.

Title: **Proposal Title: Framework for a Global Carbon Tax to Combat Climate Change**
**Preface:**  
Recognizing the urgent need to address climate change, this proposal establishes a fair, effective, and flexible global carbon tax to accelerate global greenhouse gas (GHG) reductions. This framework is grounded in the principles of equity, transparency, and collective responsibility, while incorporating differentiated approaches to reflect national circumstances.

**Action Items:**

A universal minimum carbon tax of $50 per metric ton of CO₂-equivalent emissions will be introduced by 2028, rising to $100 by 2035. Developed nations will implement the tax immediately, while developing nations will adhere to a graduated timeline, with initial phases focused on capacity-building and alignment by 2032. This tax applies to high-emitting industries, with revenues retained domestically for climate action and economic transition.

10% of global carbon tax revenues will flow into a dedicated Green Climate Fund. This fund will provide financial support, technology transfers, and capacity-building to developing nations, enabling them to adopt low-carbon technologies, enhance resilience, and transition equitably. Disbursements will be prioritized based on vulnerability and need.  

3. **Global Accountability and Flexibility Mechanisms:**  
To balance ambition with national contexts, an international oversight body will ensure transparency and compliance. Countries may adopt equivalent carbon pricing mechanisms (e.g., emissions trading systems) if they achieve comparable reductions. A review mechanism every five years will allow adjustment based on scientific developments and economic impacts.  

**Conclusion:**  
This structured, equitable approach respects historical responsibility, supports economic growth in developing nations, and promotes global cooperation toward climate resilience. We urge all nations to unite under this framework to secure a sustainable future.
